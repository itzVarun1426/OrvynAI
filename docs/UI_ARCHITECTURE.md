# UI Architecture

## Design Guidelines & Component Patterns
- **Design Tokens**: Slate 900/950 dark theme foundation with emerald/primary highlights, soft glassmorphism borders (`border-slate-800`).
- **Interactive Interview Chamber**:
  - Live candidate video feed with recording pulse indicator.
  - Center stage dynamic AI question presentation with audio synthesis and waveform visualization.
  - Live streaming transcription sidebar (Web Speech API).
  - Floating controls for microphone toggle and answer progression.
- **Visual Analytics Suite**:
  - Dashboard score card with gauge circle.
  - Multi-attribute score cards with comparative rating.
  - Skill heatmap bar indicators.
  - Accordion question breakdown featuring "Why was I asked this?" pill.
  - Interactive "Practice My Weak Areas" button.
